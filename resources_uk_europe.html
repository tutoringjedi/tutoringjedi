United Kingdom

Key UCAS deadlines, university news, and trends for students applying to UK universities.

UCAS Deadlines

Key Date

UCAS Equal Consideration Deadline: January 13, 2027

The UCAS equal consideration deadline for 2027 entry is January 13, 2027 (18:00 UK time). Applying before this date ensures all universities consider your application equally. Applications submitted after this date are considered at universities' discretion, and the final deadline for 2027 entry applications is September 23, 2027.

Oxford & Cambridge

Oxbridge & Medicine: October 15, 2026 Early Deadline

Applications for Oxford, Cambridge, and most medicine, dentistry, and veterinary courses must reach UCAS by 18:00 UK time on October 15, 2026. Most Cambridge applicants must also complete the My Cambridge Application by October 22, and the first ESAT and TMUA test windows run October 12–16. Last year's early-deadline cycle drew a record 41,010 UK 18-year-old applicants, up 5.3%.

Record Numbers

Record 338,940 UK 18-Year-Olds Applied by January 2026 Deadline

UCAS data shows 338,940 UK 18-year-olds applied by the January 2026 deadline — a record, up 4.8% on the year before. Total applicants rose 3.1% to about 619,000, and international applicants rose 5.1% to about 124,800. By the June deadline, 695,740 students had applied, up from 665,070 in 2025.

UK Trends & Policy

Visa Policy

UK Graduate Route Shortened to 18 Months From January 2027

The UK's post-study Graduate visa is being cut from two years to 18 months for applications made from January 1, 2027, while PhD graduates keep three years. Most students starting a bachelor's or master's now will graduate under the shorter rule. A £925-per-student levy on international students at English universities is also planned from the 2028/29 academic year.

Fees & Funds

England's Home Fee Cap Rises to £9,790 — International Fees Vary Widely

The maximum tuition fee for home students in England rises to £9,790 in 2026–27 at providers with a TEF award (£9,525 without), a 2.71% increase. International fees are set by each university and vary widely by course. Student visa applicants must show £1,529 per month (London) or £1,171 per month (elsewhere) for living costs under the rules in place since November 2025.

Clearing

Record 262,820 UK 18-Year-Olds Placed for 2026 — 26,000+ Courses in Clearing

A record 262,820 UK 18-year-olds secured university places for the 2026 academic year, driven largely by a growing 18-year-old population. More than 26,000 courses were available through UCAS Clearing, which stays open until October 19, 2026 for 2026 entry. Clearing remains a strong route for many students.

Rankings & Top Universities

QS Rankings 2027

QS 2027: Imperial #2 Worldwide — ETH Zurich Top Continental University

In the QS World University Rankings 2027, released in June 2026, Imperial College London ranks 2nd globally (tied with Stanford), followed by Oxford (4th) and Cambridge (6th). ETH Zurich and UCL are tied at 8th, making ETH the highest-ranked university in continental Europe. TUM (25th) and PSL (34th) lead the rankings for Germany and France.

ETH Zurich

ETH Zurich — Top-8 STEM at About CHF 4,380/Year International Tuition

ETH Zurich tripled tuition for newly enrolled international students from autumn 2025 to CHF 2,190 per semester (about CHF 4,380 a year), still low for a top-10 university, though Zurich's living costs are high. Undergraduate instruction is primarily in German; graduate programs are largely English-taught. Excellence fellowships available for top international applicants.

Germany

Germany Is Still Mostly Tuition-Free — But TUM and Baden-Württemberg Now Charge Non-EU Students

Most German public universities, including LMU Munich and Humboldt Berlin, still charge international students no tuition, only a semester contribution. TUM now charges newly enrolled non-EU students per-programme fees (roughly €2,000–3,000 per semester for bachelor's and €4,000–6,000 for master's), and Baden-Württemberg universities such as Heidelberg charge non-EU students €1,500 per semester. Most programs require strong German, and English-taught master's programs are widely available.

Scholarships & Funding

Erasmus Mundus

Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degrees — Most 2027 Deadlines Fall October–February

Erasmus Mundus scholarships cover full tuition plus a monthly living allowance for study across multiple European universities. There is no single application: each programme sets its own deadline, and most for the 2027 intake fall between October 2026 and February 2027. Highly competitive — you can apply to up to three programmes per year across STEM, social sciences, humanities, and more.

DAAD Germany

DAAD Scholarships Fund Study & Research in Germany for Internationals

The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) offers scholarships for study and research stays at German universities. Programs available at undergraduate, master's, and doctoral levels. Apply 6–12 months before your intended start date.

France

Sciences Po Emile Boutmy Scholarship — Tuition Support for Non-EU Students

Sciences Po's Emile Boutmy Scholarship supports first-time non-EU applicants admitted to undergraduate or master's programs, and you select it within the Sciences Po application rather than applying separately. Master's deadlines fall in late November and undergraduate deadlines in January, so confirm exact dates on Sciences Po's site. PSL University (which includes ENS) ranks 34th in QS 2027 and is known for highly selective admissions.

Country Highlights

Netherlands

Netherlands Moves to Restrict English-Taught Bachelor's Programs

Dutch lawmakers are advancing a bill that would require most bachelor's programmes to be taught in Dutch unless they qualify for exemptions, and universities are already capping enrolment in English-taught tracks. Master's programmes are largely exempt, and about three quarters are currently fully English-taught. Check each programme's language of instruction and enrolment limits before applying.

Switzerland

EPFL Matches ETH's CHF 2,190 International Tuition — Strong in Engineering & Life Sciences

EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) charges newly arriving international students the same CHF 2,190 per semester as ETH Zurich and offers competitive excellence fellowships for top master's and doctoral students. Programs in computer science, robotics, and life sciences are world-renowned.

Italy & Spain

Bocconi Merit Awards Can Waive Full Tuition; IE Offers Merit Scholarships Up to 50%

Bocconi University (Milan) offers a Merit Award with a full tuition waiver (worth up to about €12,000 per year for undergraduates) and an International Award with a 50% tuition reduction, both considered from your admission application. IE University (Madrid) awards merit scholarships covering up to 50% of tuition for strong academic and leadership profiles.
