Top Global Study Destinations

Key information for students considering world-class universities outside the traditional English-speaking destinations.

🇸🇬 Singapore

NUS & NTU

NUS Ranks #10 Globally in QS 2027 — Still Asia's Top University

The National University of Singapore (NUS, 10th) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU, 12th) both rank among the top 15 universities in the QS World University Rankings 2027. Both accept GMAT/GRE for graduate programs. Undergraduate admission for international students is highly selective — for AY2026/27 the application window for international qualifications ran from December 3, 2025 to February 23, 2026, and a similar December–February window is expected for AY2027/28, so confirm dates with NUS.

Scholarships

NUS Scholarships for International Undergraduates — Apply After Your Admission Application

NUS offers scholarships for international undergraduates, including the Global Merit Scholarship, the International Undergraduate Scholarship, and the Science & Technology Undergraduate Scholarship, plus faculty-specific awards. Awards vary, and the top awards cover tuition along with living support. Submit your admission application first, then your scholarship application by NUS's stated deadline; shortlisted candidates may be interviewed.

MBA Singapore

NUS MBA Ranked #1 in Asia by QS — Strong GMAT/GRE Scores Still Key

NUS Business School's MBA was ranked first in Asia for the third year running in the QS Global MBA Rankings 2026. The median GMAT for admitted students is roughly 660–680, and both the GMAT and GRE are accepted. NTU's Nanyang MBA is also highly regarded in the region, and both programs offer strong finance, tech, and Asia-Pacific business networks.

🇭🇰 Hong Kong

HKU & HKUST

HKU (#11), CUHK (#18) & HKUST (#33) Rise Among Asia's Top Universities

In the QS World University Rankings 2027, HKU ranks 11th, CUHK 18th, and HKUST 33rd, with five Hong Kong universities in the global top 100. All offer English-taught programs at undergraduate and graduate levels. HKU accepts international applications for September 2027 entry through its non-JUPAS admissions scheme (latest submission date August 25, 2027), but places are limited, so apply early.

Graduate Programs

Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme — HK$344,400/Year, Apply by December 1, 2026

The Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS) 2027/28 round is open, with 400 fellowships offering a HK$344,400 annual stipend plus a conference and research travel allowance for up to three years. Awards are made to outstanding students regardless of country of origin or academic discipline. The initial application and the university applications are both due by December 1, 2026.

MBA Hong Kong

HKUST & HKU MBA Programs Accept GMAT and GRE Scores

HKUST Business School's MBA programs are ranked #1 in Hong Kong by the Financial Times, according to the school, and require a satisfactory GMAT or GRE score. Waivers may be available on some programs for candidates with 7+ years of full-time work experience. HKU's MBA focuses on China business and is popular with students targeting Asian careers in finance and consulting.

🇯🇵 Japan

Top Universities

UTokyo's PEAK Program Has Closed — New English-Taught College of Design Opens September 2027

The University of Tokyo (39th in QS 2027) took its final intake for the PEAK English-taught undergraduate program for September 2026. It is being replaced by the new UTokyo College of Design, a five-year combined bachelor's/master's program taught entirely in English that begins in September 2027 (details still under development). Kyoto University ranks 64th, and both universities offer English-taught graduate programs.

MEXT Scholarship

Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship — Full Funding; Next Call Expected Spring 2027

The MEXT (Monbukagakusho) Scholarship covers tuition, return airfare, and a monthly allowance — ¥117,000 for undergraduates and about ¥143,000–145,000 for research and graduate students. Apply through the Japanese embassy or consulate for your country of citizenship. Embassy applications for 2027 enrollment closed in spring 2026, so students planning for 2028 should watch for the next call in spring 2027. Competition is high and strong academic records are essential.

English Programs

Growing Number of English-Taught Programs in Japan

Japanese universities are rapidly expanding English-taught programs to attract international talent. Waseda, Keio, Osaka, and Tohoku universities all offer strong English-medium graduate programs in STEM, business, and social sciences. The EJU exam is required for most applicants to Japanese-taught undergraduate programs.

🇦🇪 Middle East

UAE

NYU Abu Dhabi & AUS — World-Class Education With Need-Based Aid for All Citizenships

NYU Abu Dhabi accepts financial aid applications from all students regardless of citizenship. Aid is need-based (through the CSS Profile) and announced with the admission decision, and every admitted student is also considered for limited merit-based support. Applications go through the Common App, with the Early Decision I deadline on November 1. American University of Sharjah (AUS) offers strong STEM and business programs.

Saudi Arabia

KAUST Offers Fully Funded Graduate Programs in STEM — Fall 2027 Deadline January 3, 2027

King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) in Saudi Arabia automatically considers every admitted MS and PhD student for its fellowship, which covers tuition, housing, health insurance, and a stipend, with no separate fellowship application. The Fall 2027 application round closes January 3, 2027. All instruction is in English, GRE scores are optional or program-dependent, and strong research experience is essential.

Qatar

Education City Qatar — Georgetown Qatar Now Accepts the Common App

Qatar Foundation's Education City hosts branch campuses of Carnegie Mellon, Georgetown, Northwestern, Cornell, and other top US universities, so students can earn degrees from these institutions while based in Doha. Georgetown University in Qatar began accepting the Common App for the Fall 2027 cycle, with Early Action due November 1, 2026 and Regular Decision due January 15, 2027. Financial support options vary by campus.

🌏 Other Notable Destinations

South Korea

Seoul National University (#38) & KAIST (#65) — Fully Funded GKS Scholarships

The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) offers full funding for undergraduate and graduate students at top Korean universities including Seoul National University, KAIST, and POSTECH. The 2027 undergraduate embassy-track deadline was September 30, 2026, the university track follows each university's own schedule, and graduate embassy-track calls typically open around February. Undergraduate applicants must be under 25.

China

Chinese Government Scholarship — Full Funding at Peking (#13), Tsinghua (#14) & More

The Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) offers full tuition, accommodation, and stipend for international students at Chinese universities including Peking University, Tsinghua, and Fudan. Deadlines vary by embassy and university and typically fall between February and April for September enrollment. Starting with the 2026/27 cycle, undergraduate applicants must also take the China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA), and strong academic records are required.

Ireland

Trinity College Dublin (#75) & UCD (#100) — English-Language Study Inside the EU

Ireland's top universities — Trinity College Dublin, University College Dublin, and UCC — offer English-language instruction with EU access. EU applicants and Irish Leaving Certificate holders apply through the CAO, which opens November 5, 2026 and has a standard closing date of February 1, 2027, while non-EU applicants generally apply directly to each university. September intake is primary.
